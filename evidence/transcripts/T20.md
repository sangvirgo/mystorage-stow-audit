## Customer message

Can I collect items from the Nhon Trach warehouse at 11pm on Sunday? Please distinguish warehouse access, delivery requests, and support hours, with source links.

## Stow response

No, you cannot collect items directly from our Nhơn Trạch warehouse at 11:00 PM on Sunday.

Here is the clear distinction between warehouse access, delivery requests, and customer support hours:

1. Warehouse Access
Nhơn Trạch Warehouse (Valet / Shared Storage): This is a managed, secure facility not open for walk-in or after-hours self-service. Customers generally do not need to visit; items are retrieved via scheduled delivery. Any permitted on-site visit must be arranged and confirmed in advance during regular warehouse working hours.
Self-Storage Facilities: If you need round-the-clock access (including 11:00 PM on Sundays), our private Self-Storage units (e.g., 375 Võ Nguyên Giáp, An Phú) provide 24/7 pin/app access for active renters.
2. Delivery & Item Retrieval Requests
Advance Notice: Valet storage retrieval and delivery require at least 2 days' notice.
Scheduling: Deliveries are primarily scheduled during standard operating days (Monday to Saturday). Sunday or late-night operations are outside regular hours, subject to team availability/approval, and incur an off-hours surcharge.
3. Customer Support Hours
Operating Hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Closed on Sundays).
Inquiries or retrieval requests sent late at night or on Sunday are processed when customer service reopens on the next working day.
